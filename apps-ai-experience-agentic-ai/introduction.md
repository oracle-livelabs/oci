# Introduction

**Day in an AI Assisted Life:** Automate Tasks, Drive Efficiency, and Improve Decision-Making with AI

## About this AI Experience

An operations VP at Seer leads her team through a high-stakes pursuit for a new gym expansion. After a successful client meeting and verbal award, the team turns to Oracle Fusion’s embedded AI tools to move quickly from opportunity to execution. Sales leaders monitor pipeline activity and order progress through Sales Command Center, while procurement evaluates supplier options in Sourcing Command Center. The Quote to Requisition Agent converts project needs into actionable requests, Manager Concierge Workspace helps leaders resolve team actions, and the Payables Agent and Ledger Agent streamline invoices, payments, and financial updates so the project advances confidently and efficiently.
